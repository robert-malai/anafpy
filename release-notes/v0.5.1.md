# anafpy 0.5.1 — read invoices with any contact e-mail

## Fix

**Downloaded invoices whose contact e-mail contains `_`, `-` or `+` are readable again.** The authoring models' e-mail shape allowed only alphanumerics and dots on either side of the `@`, so an invoice ANAF had itself validated at filing — one carrying `look_contab@yahoo.com` — could not be read back: `read_invoice` raised, `DownloadedMessage.view` fell to `None`, and every field projected from that tier (number, issue date, totals, partner) silently went missing. `contact@firma-x.ro` and `a+b@gmail.com` failed the same way.

The shape had been borrowed from the wrong service: it mirrored the e-Transport Schematron's `RO-EMAIL-REGEX`, which is a declared-but-never-referenced `let` there — ANAF enforces it in **neither** service, and nothing in e-Factura constrains BT-43/58 beyond its 100-character length. It is now a permissive check that refuses only what is unambiguously malformed: a missing or duplicated `@`, whitespace, an empty side, or a leading or trailing dot on either side.

No other module was affected — e-Transport has no contact fields at all.
