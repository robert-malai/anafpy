# anafpy 0.6.1 — a buyer that isn't registered for VAT keeps its CIF

## Fix

**The buyer's CIF is no longer parsed and thrown away.** A buyer below the VAT-registration threshold — a PFA, an ÎI, a micro-entity, which is a large share of Romanian invoice traffic — has no VAT identifier, and RO e-Factura identifies it by the bare CIF in `PartyTaxScheme/CompanyID` under a non-VAT scheme marker. The reader extracted that value for every party and then discarded it for anyone who wasn't the seller, because `Party` was modelled strictly on EN 16931, whose buyer block runs BT-44..49 and has no counterpart to the seller's BT-32.

CIUS-RO reuses the slot anyway, and says so: BR-RO-120 identifies the buyer by `PartyLegalEntity/CompanyID` **or any** `PartyTaxScheme/CompanyID`, with no VAT-scheme filter — unlike BR-AE-02 and BR-IC-02 sitting right beside it, which do filter. Standard purity was costing real data on ordinary documents. `tax_registration_id` now lives on `Party`, so it survives on the buyer as well as the seller: `DownloadedMessage.view` carries it, a read → render round-trip keeps it, and an invoice to a customer under the threshold can finally be authored at all.

**`validate()` no longer fails a document ANAF accepted.** The same blind spot had BR-RO-120 hand-translated as "BT-47 and/or BT-48" — narrower than the Schematron it ports. A received invoice whose buyer was identified only by its CIF came back a fatal finding, which meant `render_invoice` refused it and `upload_invoice` failed closed: you could not re-file, or draft a credit note from, a document the system had already accepted. The rule now reads the third limb too.

Two smaller things ride along. A new `tax_registration_scheme` keeps whatever marker the source document used, so ingesting and re-rendering an invoice reproduces the issuer's own bytes; when you author one and leave it unset, the renderer writes `FC` for the seller's BT-32 — unchanged from every invoice filed so far — and `!VAT` for the buyer, which is what RO issuers file. And the "seller-only terms" finding narrowed to BT-33, where it belongs (UBL-CR-244 genuinely does forbid `CompanyLegalForm` on the customer party), and now reports that rule id instead of a bare `UBL-CR`.

Nothing else moved: BR-AE-02 and BR-IC-02 still demand a real VAT identifier from the buyer, because their Schematron limbs are VAT-schemed.

Reported as issue #7, with a production archive behind it — six of nine received invoices, from six unrelated issuers, all accepted by ANAF at filing.

## Not yet verified

**No live `validare` run confirms the new render path.** The evidence that ANAF accepts this shape is inbound: six independent issuers filed it successfully. anafpy now writes the same shape, and the round-trip that re-renders an ingested document is byte-identical to what those issuers filed — but an invoice *authored* to a non-VAT-registered buyer, marker and all, has not been put through the server-side validator or filed to TEST. `test_efactura_roundtrip_live.py` covers that on demand.
